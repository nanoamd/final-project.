# Which UK terms Kaiku can realistically win

Damien asked for the terms we could actually rank for — real volume, real
competition — rather than another round of me guessing at topics.

This is that list. Two sources: Search Console filtered to **United Kingdom +
`search_type: web`** for the 30 days to 16 September 2026, and the live SERP for
each candidate, read rather than assumed.

> **The country value is `United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
Ireland`.** Filtering on `"United Kingdom"` returns an empty set, silently.
> Anyone re-running this must use the long form or they will conclude the site
> has no UK traffic at all.

### The seven position-1 terms are worth nothing

We rank first in the UK for `paper mache table lamp`, `wood arched window
mirror`, `outdoor infrared sauna`, `computer desk with drawers and printer
space`, `how many light sources should a room have`. One impression each. Those
are not wins waiting to be converted; they are terms with no volume. Ranking
first for a term nobody searches produces nothing, and the position-1 count is
the most misleading number in Search Console.

## 2. What the SERPs actually look like

I read page one for each candidate class rather than inferring difficulty.
Three distinct patterns, and only one of them is ours.

### Winnable — "what size / how high / how many" homeware questions

`coffee table size guide` — page one is
[Funky Chunky Furniture](https://funky-chunky-furniture.co.uk/blogs/inspiration-ideas/coffee-table-size-guide-how-to-choose-the-right-size-coffee-table),
[FCI London](https://www.fcilondon.co.uk/blog/what-size-should-a-coffee-table-be),
[Reclaim Nation](https://reclaimnation.co.uk/blogs/news/the-complete-guide-to-coffee-table-dimensions-and-placement),
[Willow & Kole](https://www.willowandkole.co.uk/blogs/news/what-size-coffee-table-do-i-need-uk-size-guide),
[Evelyn Lily Interiors](https://evelynlilyinteriors.co.uk/coffee-table-heights-and-size-guide/) and
[marvinbucknell.co.uk](https://marvinbucknell.co.uk/most-popular-coffee-table-size-a-guide-to-picking-the-right-fit),
with [Wayfair](https://www.wayfair.co.uk/sca/ideas-and-advice/rooms/coffee-table-size-guide-T4226)
and [Castlery](https://www.castlery.com/uk/blog/coffee-table-dimensions) the only
large sites present.

`what size mirror above a console table` is weaker still —
[Tom Faulkner](https://www.tomfaulkner.co.uk/an-illustrated-guide-to-pairing-console-tables-mirrors/),
[Spry Interior](https://www.spryinterior.com/blogs/news/what-size-mirror-over-console-table),
[Tribesigns](https://tribesigns.uk/blogs/furniture-knowledge/uk-mirror-height-console-table-guide),
[Antique Glass](https://antiqueglassltd.co.uk/pick-right-size-mirror/),
[Petalwood Interiors](https://petalwoodinteriors.co.uk/blogs/furniture-blog/what-is-the-rule-for-a-console-table)
and [LED Mirror World](https://ledmirrorworld.com/blogs/news/what-size-mirror-over-console-table) —
one page even belongs to a Chinese mirror factory.

**These are Shopify blog posts from retailers our own size.** No national
publisher, no John Lewis, no Ideal Home. This is the one class of UK term where
Kaiku, with zero backlinks, is not outgunned on authority — and where a
calculator that answers the question rather than a blog post that describes it
is a genuinely better result.

### Not winnable — product terms

`teak corner shelf` returns
[eBay UK](https://www.ebay.co.uk/b/bn_3269527),
[Wayfair](https://www.wayfair.co.uk/keyword.php?keyword=teak+corner+shelves),
three [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/market/teak_wood_corner_shelf) category
pages, and two specialists. Marketplace category pages carrying thousands of
listings each. We sit at position 22 for `teak wood corner shelf` and there is
no realistic path from 22 to 1 against eBay and Etsy without links we do not
have.

The same applies to `silver skeleton clock` (40), `beer barrel table` (53),
`bloom garden furniture` (38 — another company's brand) and `large karlsson
clock` (27 — a brand we do not stock). **Every striking-distance term in the
20–60 band is a product term, and every product term is a marketplace SERP.**
The band that looks most promising on a position report is the one worth least.

### Not winnable — publisher topics

`how to keep a garden warm` returns [Gardening Know
How](https://www.gardeningknowhow.com/garden-how-to/info/5-ways-to-stay-warm.htm),
[LoveProperty](https://www.loveproperty.com/news/102327/outdoor-heating-7-best-ways-to-warm-your-garden-all-year-round),
[Goodhomes Magazine](https://www.goodhomesmagazine.com/garden/outdoor-heating-options/),
two AOL syndications and
[Harbour Lifestyle](https://www.harbourlifestyle.co.uk/blogs/news/outdoor-heating-ideas).

I wrote a 1,694-word guide on 15 September aimed at ranking first for this term.
**That was a bad target and I should have checked the SERP before writing it.**
The guide is good and it will not reach page one against national publishers at
our current authority. It stays — it is useful to anyone who lands on it — but it
is not a traffic plan.

### Deprioritise — `plant pot sizes`

Page one is [RHS Plants](https://www.rhsplants.co.uk/html/pot_size.htm),
[Paramount Plants](https://www.paramountplants.co.uk/pot-size-guide.html),
[Jacksons Nurseries](https://www.jacksonsnurseries.co.uk/plant-pot-size-guide.html)
and [Johnsons Nurseries](https://johnsonsnurseries.co.uk/solutions/plant-pot-size-guide/) —
nurseries with deep topical authority. Worse, **the intent is wrong for us**: that
search wants the litres-to-centimetres conversion for nursery pots, and we sell
decorative planters. Winning it would bring traffic that cannot buy anything.

## 3. "kaiku" at position 36 is not a problem to fix

The most alarming-looking number in the data is that we sit at **position 36 in
the UK for our own name**, on 5 impressions and 1 click.

Page one for `kaiku` belongs to Kaiku Corporación Alimentaria (a Basque dairy
group), Kaiku Health (a Finnish oncology app), a former Spanish pro cycling team,
a Spotify artist and a Wikipedia disambiguation page. The 30 impressions from
Denmark and 18 from Canada are people looking for those, not for us.

**`kaiku` is not our brand term and never will be. `kaiku home` and `kaikuhome`
are.** Chasing the bare word means competing with a dairy corporation for traffic
that would bounce. The `alternateName` work already shipped (`Kaiku Home`,
`kaikuhome`, `Kaiku Home Store`) is the right and sufficient response.

The `kaikoo` cluster — 34 UK impressions a month at positions 33–42 — is a
different British furniture brand whose name resembles ours. We are being shown
for it by accident. **No action**: building content to intercept another
company's brand searches is not something I will do, and the traffic would not
convert anyway.

## 5. What this does and does not promise

Winning every term in the list above does not produce thousands of monthly
visitors. These are questions with modest volume, and the honest ceiling on this
cluster is hundreds of UK sessions a month, not thousands.

It is, however, the only class of term reachable **without links**, which is what
makes it the right place to start. The links remain the binding constraint on
everything else, and nothing in this document changes that.
