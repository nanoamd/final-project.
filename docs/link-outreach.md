# Link outreach — the half that decides whether any of this ranks

The pages are now deep enough to rank. They will not, without links.

This is the list, the emails, and the order to work them in. Every target below
is a real URL I checked, not a category of site.

> **Send these from Damien's own address, as Damien.** Not from a tool, not on a
> schedule, and not fifty at a time. Ten personal emails beat two hundred
> templated ones, and the templated route is how a new domain acquires a spam
> signal instead of a link.

## What not to do

Every one of these will cost more than it returns, and some carry a penalty risk:

- **Buying links.** Directly against Google's spam policies, and the sites
  selling them are the sites already flagged for it.
- **Guest post networks and "we'll place you on DA50 sites" emails.** Same thing
  with an invoice attached. You will get these once outreach starts.
- **Bulk directory submissions.** Hundreds of low-quality listings is a
  recognised spam pattern, not a shortcut.
- **Automated outreach tools.** A templated sequence from a new domain earns a
  spam reputation before it earns a link.
- **Reciprocal link swaps with other shops.** Small scale, obvious pattern, and
  you are linking to a competitor.

The one exception on directories: a genuine, relevant, human-reviewed listing —
a local business directory for your town, or a trade body — is fine. It is the
volume that makes it spam, not the format.

## What to expect

Honest numbers, not encouragement.

- Tier 1 is the highest hit rate on the list. Six emails, and a supplier has a
  real reason to say yes.
- Tier 2, expect roughly one in four to five.
- Tier 3, expect roughly one in ten, and expect most of the replies to be no.

Twenty-five emails across the three tiers is a realistic weekend's work and
should produce somewhere between five and ten links. On SERPs held by
Funky Chunky Furniture and Petalwood Interiors rather than by national
publishers, that is a meaningful shift rather than a rounding error.

**It is not instant.** Links take weeks to be crawled and months to be
reflected in position. Nothing here shows up before the new year.

```
/tools/coffee-table-size-calculator
/tools/mirror-size-calculator
/tools/pendant-light-size-calculator
/tools/sofa-size-calculator
/tools/dining-table-size-calculator
/tools/tv-unit-size-calculator
/tools/vase-size-calculator
/tools/wall-clock-size-calculator
/learn/coffee-table-size-guide
```

626 pages on this site are "Discovered – not indexed". **A page that is not
indexed cannot rank at any position, and no number of links changes that.**
This costs nothing and it gates everything else on the page.
