12 August 2026. Written because the 20-supplier outreach list is not getting
replies, and because deleting the Aosom range has left 19 of 41 categories with
nothing in them.

---

## Why nobody is replying

It is not the email. It is the ask.

Every name on `supplier-targets-20.md` is a premium brand with an existing
stockist network — Gozney, Kamado Joe, Garden Trading, Libra Interiors. When a
brand like that receives an approach from a site with no trading history, no
order volume and no reviews, the honest internal answer is "come back when you
have customers", and the usual outward answer is silence. That is not a
rejection of Kaiku. It is what happens to every new retailer, and it does not
change until there is something to point at.

So the sequence has to be the other way round:

1. **Get stock from suppliers whose business model is onboarding new retailers.**
   Approval is automated or near-automated, because their revenue depends on
   volume of retailers, not on curating them.
2. **Sell things.** Even at £15. That produces order history, reviews and a
   trading record.
3. **Then go back to the premium brands** with numbers. The same email that gets
   silence today reads completely differently with "we turned over £X last
   quarter and hold 4.8 stars across N reviews".

The 20-supplier list is not wasted. It is the month-three list.

## What you actually asked for: low to high

You want placeholder products to fill empty categories _and_ premium products, so
the price range runs low to high. That is the right instinct and it lines up with
`first-sale-plan.md`, which found the catalogue's cheapest item is £6.95 and
concluded the first sale will be a cheap one. Nobody's first order from an unknown
shop is a £1,095 sofa.

It also fixes a live problem: **an empty category converts at exactly zero.**
Right now that is every Lighting category, Water Features, Garden Lighting and
Fire Pits.

One caution, stated once. Marketplace and dropship stock is generic — the same
products appear on hundreds of sites. Used carelessly it will undo the premium
positioning that is the whole point of Kaiku. So:

- Use it to **fill categories that are currently empty**, not to pad ones that
  already work.
- Keep the **homepage and the New & Noteworthy rail on the premium lines** —
  D.I. Designs, SaunaPlunge.
- Still write the descriptions yourself. The rule has not changed.

## What to do with each account once you have it

Short version, because the accounts are the easy part and this is where it
usually goes wrong.

1. **Take one product per empty category first**, not fifty. A category with
   three good products beats one with thirty generic ones, and it tells you
   whether the supplier's photography is usable before you commit.
2. **Check the images before the price.** The audit in `docs/image-audit.md`
   found nine unusable images in the current catalogue. A supplier whose photos
   are 800×800 will look soft on a phone, and that is not fixable afterwards.
3. **Set your own price.** Standing instruction, unchanged: prices are yours, no
   feed imports.
4. **Write the description yourself.** Also unchanged. The validator in
   `scripts/lib/product-copy.ts` will refuse banned phrases and duplicate FAQ
   answers, so copied supplier wording will not pass.
5. **Record the supplier and the source URL in Sanity** as you go. Three wrong
   `sourceUrl`s have already had to be corrected, two of which pointed at a
   basket page.
