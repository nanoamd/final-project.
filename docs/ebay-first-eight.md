# List these eight by hand first

eBay gates bulk upload behind one completed sale: _"Private sellers just need
to have made at least one sale to get access."_ So the Upload tab does not exist
yet, and the way to it is one sale.

These are the cheapest products with real stock behind them — lowest price means
fastest sale, and a fast sale is the whole point here rather than the margin.
Everything below is copy-paste ready.

Let eBay suggest the category as you list each one, and **write the category ID down**
— it is in the URL. Eight of these gives most of the nineteen IDs the bulk file needs.
