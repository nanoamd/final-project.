is brand-direct to authorised dealers, and the wholesalers who do carry BBQs carry
the cheap end. So the route into that category is a **dealership application to the
brand itself**, which is a different and slower conversation: they will ask about
showroom, service capability and volume.

## What to expect, so the replies do not read as failure

Of thirteen applications sent on 8 August, one accepted and several declined. That
is a normal hit rate for a business with no trading history — it is not a verdict on
Kaiku.

Three things shift it:

1. **Dropship suppliers say yes more often.** They carry the stock risk either way,
   so a new account costs them almost nothing. Numbers 1, 5 and 6 are the highest
   probability on this list, and number 1 is the highest value.
2. **Ask for the smallest thing first.** "Can I see your trade price list" gets
   answered far more often than "can I open an account".
3. **A supplier who declines has told you their condition.** Auroom's objection is
   almost certainly installation capability, not size. Write the condition down and
   diary the re-approach.
